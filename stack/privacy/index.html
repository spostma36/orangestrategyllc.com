Privacy Policy

Stack: Golf Side Games · Orange Strategy LLC · Last updated November 18, 2026

The short version: Stack has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone (except a course name you search for, which goes to a free course directory; see Course search), and we (Orange Strategy LLC) don't receive it. The only things that can reach us are emails you choose to send and anonymous crash reports from Apple if you've turned those on.

What stays on your phone

Player names and nicknames, handicap indexes, which course ratings each player uses, mobile numbers you add, courses and tees, scores, game settings, saved Stacks, event names and round history are stored only on your device. Estimated handicaps are worked out on your iPhone from rounds you've logged in Stack. We don't have servers, accounts, or a copy of anything you enter.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't have any

Camera and photos

If you scan a scorecard, by taking a photo or choosing one from Photos, the picture is read on your iPhone to fill in pars and handicaps. It isn't uploaded or saved by Stack. The same goes for your group's written rules when Stack reads them for you (a photo, screenshot, spreadsheet or text): they're read on your iPhone, on iPhones with Apple Intelligence by Apple's on-device model, and only the Stack you save is kept. If you save a round image to Photos, Stack can only add that image; it can't see your photo library.

Course search

If you tap Find this course, the course name you type (and the course you pick from its list) is sent to OpenGolfAPI (opengolfapi.org), a free course directory, which sends back that course's tees, ratings and holes. Nothing else goes with it: no names, handicaps, scores or rounds. Like any website, OpenGolfAPI sees your iPhone's internet address; its own privacy policy covers what it keeps. We never receive your searches. Course data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0) via OpenGolfAPI.

Contacts and phone numbers

You may add friends' names, handicaps and mobile numbers; only add information they're comfortable with, and delete it if they ask. You can add a player's mobile number by typing it or choosing it from your Contacts. Stack only sees the one number you pick (it never reads your whole address book), and the number stays on your iPhone. It's used only to fill in the recipients when you tap "Text the group." You review and send the text yourself in Messages.

When you share

Sharing only happens when you tap Share image or Text the group on a finished round, or start Follow along (below). You choose what the round image shows (the scorecard and each game's details). The image, and a link that lets friends who have Stack save the finished round, go only to the people and apps you choose. Once sent, it's in those people's messages, outside Stack. Send course puts only a course's name, tees, ratings and holes in a link you send.

Follow along

When a scorekeeper starts Follow along, the round's player names, handicaps, scores, games and amounts go to the iPhones of the people who join, through Apple's SharePlay, each time a hole is saved. A link under a shared round carries the same finished results inside the link itself; the web page it may open (orangestrategyllc.com/stack/open/) passes them to Stack and doesn't send or keep them. We never receive any of it. A round you save from someone else stays on your iPhone, in Round History and your Ledger.

Backup file

Settings › Backup saves everything in Stack, including your players' phone numbers, to one file in the place you choose (Files, iCloud Drive, a computer). We never receive it. Restore reads a file you pick and stays on your iPhone.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, Stack's data is included in that backup. Backups are controlled by you and Apple, and we can't access them.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They don't identify you.

Age

Stack is for adults (18+). Stack asks Apple for your age range (never your birthday); where Apple can't answer, it asks you to confirm you're 18 or older. Stack keeps only a yes, and how it was given, on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Swipe to delete a player, course, Stack or round, or erase everything in Clubhouse › Settings › Erase all data. Deleting the app also removes it from your iPhone. Copies in existing backups stay until you delete or replace those backups.

Do Not Track

Stack doesn't track you across apps or websites, so Do Not Track signals don't change anything.

Changes

If this changes (for example, if we add accounts or other online features), we'll update this page and the app before anything else leaves your iPhone.
