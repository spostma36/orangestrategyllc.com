Privacy Policy

Stack: Golf Side Games · Orange Strategy LLC · Last updated October 16, 2026

The short version: Stack doesn't collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your phone

Player names, handicap indexes, mobile numbers you add, courses and tees, scores, game settings, saved Stacks and round history are stored only on your device. We don't have servers, accounts, or a copy of anything you enter.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't have any

Camera and photos

If you scan a scorecard, the photo is read on your iPhone to fill in pars and handicaps. It isn't uploaded or saved by Stack.

Contacts and phone numbers

You may add friends' names, handicaps and mobile numbers; only add information they're comfortable with, and delete it if they ask. You can add a player's mobile number by typing it or choosing it from your Contacts. Stack only sees the one number you pick (it never reads your whole address book), and the number stays on your iPhone. It's used only to fill in the recipients when you tap "Text the group." You review and send the text yourself in Messages.

When you share

Sharing only happens when you tap Share or Text the group. The scorecard image and results text go only to the people and apps you choose. Once sent, they're in those people's messages, outside Stack.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, Stack's data is included in that backup. Backups are controlled by you and Apple, and we can't access them.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They don't identify you.

Children

Stack isn't directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Swipe to delete a player, course, Stack or round, or erase everything in Clubhouse › Settings › Erase all data. Deleting the app also removes it from your iPhone. Copies in existing backups stay until you delete or replace those backups.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if we add accounts or online features), we'll update this page and the app before it happens.
