Stack: Golf Side Games

Getting started

  1. Add your players and their handicaps.
  2. Add your course: search for it (Clubhouse › Courses › + › Find this course) or scan the scorecard, then check what it filled in (holes marked Check need a look).
  3. Build your Stack: pick your games, set the amounts, adjust your house rules. Playing alone or in a scramble? Pick Just me or Scramble on the Stack tab.
  4. Tee off. Enter scores hole by hole, then tap Save & next hole.
  5. When the last hole is saved, open the Card tab, tap Games and tap Finish round. Once the round is finished, share the results.

Common questions

How do my friends get the results?

Sharing opens once the round is finished (Card › Games › Finish round). Tap Share image or Text the group to send the round image (Text the group also adds a link that lets friends who have Stack save the finished round); switches let you choose what it shows (the scorecard and each game's details), and Settings › Sharing sets the defaults. You can also share it later from Round History. For Text the group, add each player's mobile number in Clubhouse › The Ledger first.

Can my group watch the round on their phones?

Yes, if they have Stack. On Card › Games, tap Follow along and pick your group text. Everyone who joins sees the card and the games on their iPhone, view only, through Apple's SharePlay. When the round is finished they can tap Save to my Ledger, and if the scorekeeper fixes the round later, Update my Ledger. On a new phone, set up Stack first (your name, then your games), then tap the invite.

Can Stack read our group's rules?

Stack tab › More options › Read our rules: take a photo or choose a screenshot, spreadsheet or text of your rules. It's read on your iPhone. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Stack drafts the games for you to check; otherwise you pick the games beside your rules. Nothing is saved until you save the new Stack.

Can I send a course to a friend?

Open a saved course in Clubhouse › Courses and tap Send course. The link carries only that course's name, tees, ratings and holes; your friend taps Add to my Courses.

The scorecard scan got something wrong.

The scanner is a helper, not a perfect reader. Scorecards vary a lot. Numbers it couldn't read show as "?". After Use these values, check every number on the course before saving, and tap any number to fix it.

My group plays a game differently.

Open the game from your Stack and look through its house rules (every game's rules are also in Clubhouse › Games & House Rules). Most variations are there. If yours isn't, email us. That's exactly the kind of feedback we want.

Can I fix a hole after saving it?

Yes. Go to the hole, tap Edit hole, make the change, then tap Save changes. Stack shows how the money changes before you confirm.

Where does a player's handicap come from?

Type an index on the player's card in The Ledger and Stack uses it. With no typed index, Stack estimates one from that player's rounds in Stack once there are enough; it's an estimate, not an official handicap.

I'm getting a new iPhone. Will my history move over?

Yes, if you set up the new phone from an iCloud backup or transfer directly from your old phone. Stack's data comes along automatically. You can also save everything to one file in Clubhouse › Settings › Backup (Save a backup) and use Restore from a backup on the new phone. Restore replaces what's on that phone and keeps a copy first, so you can put it back.

Does Stack handle payments?

No. Stack only keeps score and does the math for games your group sets up. It never handles money and doesn't offer gambling. Settling up is between you and your group.

Why does Stack ask for my age?

Stack is for adults (18+). It asks Apple for your age range (never your birthday), or asks you to confirm you're 18 or older where Apple can't answer, and keeps only a yes on your iPhone.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe to delete a player, course, Stack or round, or erase everything in Clubhouse › Settings › Erase all data (not available during a round). Your iCloud backups aren't changed.
