Stack: Golf Side Games

Getting started

  1. Add your players and their handicaps.
  2. Add your course, or scan the scorecard (then check what it read).
  3. Build your Stack: pick your games, set the stakes, adjust your house rules.
  4. Tee off. Enter scores hole by hole, then tap Save & next hole.
  5. When the last hole is saved, open the Card tab, tap Games and tap Finish round. Once the round is finished, share the results.

Common questions

How do my friends get the results?

Sharing opens once the round is finished (Card › Games › Finish round). Then tap Text the group to send everyone the results card, balances and the full scorecard in one text, or share it later from Round History. Add each player's mobile number in Clubhouse › The Ledger first.

The scorecard scan got something wrong.

The scanner is a helper, not a perfect reader. Scorecards vary a lot. Always review the highlighted numbers before saving, and tap any number to fix it.

My group plays a game differently.

Open the game from your Stack and look through its House Rules. Most variations are there. If yours isn't, email us. That's exactly the kind of feedback we want.

Can I fix a hole after saving it?

Yes. Go to the hole, tap Edit hole, make the change, then tap Save changes. Stack shows how the money changes before you confirm.

I'm getting a new iPhone. Will my history move over?

Yes, if you set up the new phone from an iCloud backup or transfer directly from your old phone. Stack's data comes along automatically.

Does Stack handle payments?

No. Stack only keeps score and does the math for games your group sets up. It never handles money and doesn't offer gambling. Settling up is between you and your group.

Why does Stack ask for my age?

Stack is for adults (18+). It asks Apple for your age range (never your birthday) and keeps only a yes on your iPhone.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe to delete a player, course, Stack or round, or erase everything in Clubhouse › Settings › Erase all data (not available during a round). Your iCloud backups aren't changed.
